Common questions


Is mortgage protection different from regular life insurance?
Mortgage protection is a life insurance policy sized to your mortgage. It is not issued by your lender, and your family gets the money and decides how to use it. Many versions are term life with a term matching your loan.


Will I need a medical exam?
Some policies need no exam and decide based on your answers to health questions, sometimes the same day. Others ask for an exam or records. Coverage limits and age limits are usually lower on no-exam policies.


What’s final expense insurance?
A smaller permanent policy meant to pay for funeral costs and final bills. It is generally for older adults and usually has lower coverage amounts. Some policies have a waiting period for full benefits.


What does term life mean?
Coverage for a set period, such as 10, 20, or 30 years. If you die during the term, your family is paid. If the term ends, so does the coverage, unless the policy lets you renew or convert.
